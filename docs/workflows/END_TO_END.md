# End-to-end workflow

1. Website or permission-based conversation creates an assessment request.
2. A01 validates, deduplicates and writes Company, Contact and Lead records.
3. Owner reviews the lead and records the next action.
4. A qualified lead creates an Opportunity.
5. Proposal is generated from approved fields and reviewed manually.
6. Won opportunity triggers the client-folder and Linear-delivery workflow.
7. Service is delivered from a controlled template with QA.
8. Handover, feedback, time and margin are recorded.
9. Weekly digest reconciles CRM and Linear.
10. Each repository stage writes its report and next instruction.
