# Creixement — Kairon V10 Business Portfolio

## Executive model

Kairon is the Chief Operator of Creixement. The business is one portfolio, not a collection of peer control planes. Tectum is the first operating project inside the Business portfolio. New projects are created by the Kairon venture foundry from evidence-linked opportunities and then move through governed stages.

The cockpit therefore has six top-level operating domains: Command, Business, Operations, Evolution, Evidence and System. Project-specific surfaces live inside Business. Legacy project URLs may redirect into Business, but they do not create additional top-level authorities.

## Project lifecycle

`hypothesis → discovery → experiment → incubating → operating → scaling`

Projects may also become `paused` or `archived`. Kairon can create evidence-linked L1 recommendations. It can run bounded, internal and reversible L2 experiments only while the supervised economic L2 gate is open. It can incubate a new project only when the source reaches governed-source evidence and the stronger evidence, confidence, strategic-fit, reversibility, risk and rights thresholds pass.

Kairon cannot self-declare a foundry project `operating` or `scaling`. That transition requires at least one verified external outcome, at least one executed receipt and explicit owner approval. Forecast revenue and project scoring never count as verified commercial outcomes.

## Current portfolio

Tectum is seeded as the first owner-established operating project. Its specialist real-estate agent remains scoped to Tectum; Kairon remains the executive operator above it. Tectum underwriting, properties, cases, evidence, reports and approvals are therefore Business project operations rather than a separate control plane.

No other operating project is seeded. If the opportunity store is empty, the foundry reports zero project candidates instead of manufacturing examples.

## Authority membrane

Kairon's constitutional ceiling remains L2. Contracts, unrestricted payments, property offers or purchases, financing commitments, irreversible governed deletion, rights or consent bypass and unreceipted consequential actions remain owner-controlled L3 boundaries. The venture foundry does not weaken these rules.

Conway-inspired ecology may alter ranking, experimentation, recovery and promotion confidence. It cannot mutate the truth hierarchy, owner authority, privacy/consent, secrets, payments, contracts, property authority, financing authority, audit history, provenance, release proof or the L2 ceiling.

## Runtime evidence

The Kairon supervisory snapshot now binds runtime proof, current Conway court suite, ecological state, operating health and `v_business_portfolio_summary_v10`. Its deterministic proof digest therefore represents the business portfolio as part of Kairon's supervised operating state.

The venture foundry runs through `business.venture_foundry_cycle`, backed by `creixement_run_venture_foundry_v10()`. The RPC is service-role-only. The browser cockpit remains read-only and sees the portfolio only through member-filtered projections.
