# Creixement Autonomous Economic Brain v2

Status: implementation contract
Date: 2026-09-09
Runtime: cloud-only

## Mission

Creixement is a cloud-native venture/product factory that continuously searches for real commercial opportunities, converts evidence into products and actions, executes everything permitted by policy, measures verified outcomes, and reallocates attention toward the strongest repeatable economic paths.

The system is not one unrestricted model. It exposes one operator-facing **Chief Operator** in Lovable while internally using specialist agents, deterministic services, a policy constitution, append-only receipts and an evidence-weighted evolutionary loop.

The objective is not idea count. The objective is verified useful outcomes: qualified demand, conversion, paid revenue, contribution margin, repeat revenue, delivery quality and strategically valuable learning.

## Reused mechanisms from Conway and Pneumara

Creixement adopts the useful mechanics while keeping its own business domain and authority boundaries:

- persistent mission and scheduled autonomy;
- continuous public/approved-source opportunity sensing;
- USE / BUILD / BUY / COMBINE / ABSTAIN decisions;
- commercial genomes;
- bounded mutation and crossover;
- niches/speciation so unlike product families are not compared naively;
- short-horizon market state ('epigenetic' state);
- telomere-like exploration budgets and senescence for weak hypotheses;
- archive-not-delete for weak lineages;
- economic fitness based on verified external evidence;
- capital/attention reallocation toward winners;
- counterparty memory;
- evidence-triggered capability promotion;
- deterministic replay and audit receipts;
- constitutional controls that evolution cannot mutate;
- authorization envelopes that permit bounded automation without a human click on every low-risk action.

## Operator model

Lovable presents one **Creixement Chief Operator**. It can receive goals such as:

- "Find the best opportunities we can monetise this week."
- "Generate, test and rank three new offers from the reports we already own."
- "Find companies that fit IVA and prepare the next actions."
- "Find Tectum cases with the highest unlockable value."
- "Find funding opportunities for current clients."
- "Improve the product portfolio using observed market evidence."

The Chief Operator decomposes the goal and delegates to specialist agents. It does not bypass policy.

## Specialist agents

1. Chief Operator — persistent mission, decomposition, routing, synthesis and run ownership.
2. Opportunity Hunter — continuously discovers, deduplicates and scores commercial signals.
3. Research Agent — public/company/market research and evidence collection.
4. Product Architect — converts recurring needs and owned IP into product candidates.
5. Commercial Agent — account matching, qualification, outreach/proposal preparation and bounded campaign execution.
6. Market Intelligence Agent — market/competitor/buyer/channel intelligence.
7. Funding Agent — programme discovery, fit scoring and monitoring.
8. Tectum Agent — property opportunity intelligence and controlled cloud underwriting/report pipeline.
9. Report Factory Agent — controlled generation of reports and commercial assets.
10. Marketing Agent — positioning, content, landing pages, campaign variants and distribution plans.
11. Finance/Economics Agent — pricing, contribution margin, CAC/LTV proxies, capacity and verified revenue analysis.
12. Evolution Agent — genomes, niches, mutation/crossover, senescence, selection and budget reallocation.
13. Counterparty Agent — persistent memory of companies, investors, partners, providers and machine counterparties.
14. Experiment Agent — creates bounded experiments, success criteria and evidence collection plans.
15. QA/Critic — adversarial evidence/math/claim/readiness review.
16. Compliance/Governor — rights, consent, privacy, channel policy and constitutional enforcement.
17. Automation Engineer — schedules, connectors, retries, idempotency and health.
18. Synthesis Agent — cross-agent ranking and operator recommendations.

## Continuous opportunity loop

The autonomous loop runs on scheduled ticks and event-driven triggers:

`SENSE -> INGEST -> NORMALISE -> DEDUPE -> VERIFY RIGHTS/FRESHNESS -> CLASSIFY SIGNAL -> MATCH TO CAPABILITIES -> SCORE OPPORTUNITY -> RESEARCH -> BUILD/USE/COMBINE/ABSTAIN -> CREATE EXPERIMENT/ACTION -> POLICY CHECK -> EXECUTE AUTHORIZED ACTIONS -> VERIFY RECEIPT -> OBSERVE OUTCOME -> UPDATE FITNESS -> MUTATE/SELECT/ARCHIVE -> REPEAT`

Signal sources include, where authorized:

- CRM activity and stale pipeline;
- client requests and Gmail;
- Google Drive reports and owned IP;
- approved public web research;
- company/market enrichment providers;
- funding feeds;
- property feeds and Tectum evidence sources;
- website analytics;
- partner/referral feeds;
- GitHub/Linear implementation state;
- operator instructions;
- prior experiments and failed opportunities.

## Opportunity object

Every opportunity is structured, not narrative-only. Minimum fields:

- source signal(s) and provenance;
- target customer/counterparty;
- product/capability match;
- problem evidence;
- expected revenue band;
- expected contribution margin;
- probability/confidence;
- time-to-cash estimate;
- delivery effort;
- automation potential;
- strategic fit;
- recurrence potential;
- evidence quality/freshness;
- legal/rights dependency;
- reversible next test;
- current blocker;
- status and expiry.

No opportunity is treated as demand or revenue merely because an AI generated it.

## Economic fitness

Fitness uses verified observations, with weights configurable by niche. Default components:

- paid revenue and attributable settlement/commercial completion;
- contribution margin after direct delivery costs;
- repeat purchase / repeat usage;
- qualified replies, meetings and proposals as weaker leading indicators;
- time to conversion;
- delivery time and revision burden;
- automation percentage;
- customer outcome/acceptance evidence;
- retention/referral evidence;
- confidence and evidence quality;
- downside/risk/rights complexity.

The engine ranks real paid/accepted usage above hypotheses, and hypotheses above unsupported ideas.

## Commercial genomes

A genome is a versioned commercial strategy, not model weights. Genes can include:

- product family and product composition;
- target segment;
- geography;
- problem framing;
- channel mix;
- offer structure;
- price/discount bounds;
- SLA;
- evidence requirements;
- CTA;
- report/template variant;
- qualification thresholds;
- follow-up cadence;
- acquisition vs upsell emphasis;
- exploitation / adjacency / exploration allocation.

Genomes are assigned to niches so Tectum sourcing, funding radar and SME visibility audits do not compete under one naive fitness scale.

## Evolution mechanics

The Evolution Agent may:

- increase allocation to variants with verified superior fitness;
- reduce exploration budget for repeatedly weak variants;
- archive weak lineages without deleting their evidence;
- mutate one or a small number of bounded genes at a time;
- crossover compatible winning traits inside a niche;
- create adjacent product bundles when paired demand is repeatedly observed;
- promote a recurring manual capability into an automated capability only after successful evidence;
- restore archived variants if market state changes.

Evolution may never mutate:

- audit history;
- evidence/provenance truth;
- revenue truth;
- authentication and secret handling;
- consent/privacy requirements;
- anti-scraping/access-control rules;
- maximum spend/payment authority;
- non-delegable contract/property/payment rules;
- emergency stop / lockdown;
- report digest/approval semantics;
- cloud-only production rule.

## Exploration metabolism

Each niche receives an attention budget measured in abstract allocation units, not spendable money by default.

Default seed allocation:

- 70% exploit: improve/sell validated winners;
- 20% adjacency: test nearby segments, bundles and channels;
- 10% exploration: genuinely new products/markets.

This is a configurable seed, not a fixed law. Evidence can move the allocation within constitutional bounds.

## Authorization versus approval

Creixement distinguishes **runtime authorization** from **per-action human approval**.

An action can execute without a click when all of the following are true:

1. a real connector/execution adapter exists;
2. a versioned policy explicitly authorizes that action class;
3. the action is inside the configured envelope;
4. prerequisites/rights/consent are satisfied;
5. idempotency and audit receipts are supported;
6. no non-delegable constitutional rule applies.

Examples that can become autonomously authorized:

- public/approved-source research;
- company/market/funding/property discovery where source terms permit it;
- internal CRM updates;
- report drafts and QA;
- internal product/experiment creation;
- bounded enrichment;
- scheduled follow-up drafting;
- external outreach within an explicitly approved sender/channel/audience/rate/template/stop-rule envelope;
- low-risk content publication within an explicitly approved brand/channel/template envelope;
- low-risk product catalogue changes inside approved price/scope bounds.

Non-delegable by default:

- contracts that create material legal obligation;
- bank transfers and unrestricted payments;
- property offers/purchases;
- financing commitments;
- destructive deletion of governed evidence/client records;
- actions outside consent/rights/source terms;
- any action whose execution adapter cannot return a receipt.

## USE / BUILD / BUY / COMBINE / ABSTAIN

For every opportunity, the Chief Operator must consider:

- USE: deliver using an existing proven Creixement capability.
- BUILD: create a missing reusable capability when expected lifetime value justifies it.
- BUY: use a third-party cloud capability only if an approved procurement/spend envelope exists and economics justify it.
- COMBINE: compose existing capabilities into a new workflow/product.
- ABSTAIN: reject low-confidence, low-margin or high-risk opportunities.

Buying is disabled until a bounded procurement constitution is explicitly enabled.

## Counterparty memory

Persistent counterparty memory stores only permitted business-relevant information and provenance. It records:

- identity/entity resolution;
- relationship type;
- observed needs/interests;
- prior interactions;
- product fit;
- response and purchase history;
- reliability/quality indicators;
- consent/contact constraints;
- source/freshness;
- next useful action.

Counterparty memory is not a hidden personal-profile warehouse. Sensitive data is excluded unless a legitimate controlled workflow specifically requires it.

## Product neurogenesis

A new capability/product can be promoted from experimental to reusable only when evidence shows repeatability. Promotion gates should include:

- multiple successful executions or a strong paid client case;
- reproducible inputs/outputs;
- defined QA checks;
- known failure modes;
- cost/time measurements;
- source/rights compliance;
- positive unit economics or strategic rationale;
- versioned template/schema;
- an execution receipt.

## Truth hierarchy

1. Verified external outcome / payment / delivery acceptance.
2. Executed connector receipt.
3. Governed source/evidence record.
4. Human-approved business decision.
5. Structured model inference with evidence.
6. Hypothesis.
7. Generated narrative.

Lower levels cannot overwrite higher ones.

## Definition of complete autonomous loop

The autonomous economic brain is operational only when a cloud scheduler/event source can:

1. ingest at least one real opportunity source automatically;
2. discover and score an opportunity;
3. collect evidence;
4. select USE/BUILD/COMBINE/ABSTAIN;
5. create an experiment or action;
6. execute at least one authorized cloud action;
7. verify the result with a receipt;
8. update opportunity/genome fitness from the outcome;
9. expose the full chain in Lovable;
10. repeat without an operator machine.

The operator should intervene mainly to set constitutions, connect accounts, approve genuinely consequential classes, review exceptions, and make high-level strategic decisions.
