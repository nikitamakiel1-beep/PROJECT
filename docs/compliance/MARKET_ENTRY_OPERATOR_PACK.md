# Market-entry operator pack — Spain and Catalonia

> Status: operational preparation only. This document does not replace legal, tax, labour, municipal or data-protection advice. The owner has frozen professional privacy, legal and invoicing review. The website may remain in synthetic staging, but live intake, client personal data, proposals, invoices, payments and paid delivery remain blocked.

## 1. Current legal classification of the service menu

The planned services are digital implementation, commercial-content and business-process services. No sector-specific professional licence has been identified for the current bounded scope.

This conclusion depends on strict service boundaries:

- **International Visibility Audit** means a website/commercial-readiness review. It is not an audit of annual accounts, financial statements or accounting records.
- **CRM & Lead Tracker Setup** means configuration and workflow implementation. It does not authorise the venture to sell data, determine a client's lawful marketing basis or contact prospects autonomously.
- **International Sales One-Pager** means copywriting, localisation and evidence-controlled presentation. It is not certified legal translation or regulated-sector advertising advice.
- **WhatsApp Business Setup** means account and workflow configuration. The client must own the number/account and approve the legal basis for any communication.
- **AI-assisted recommendations** remain assistive, transparent and human-reviewed. Employment, credit, biometric and emotion-recognition decisions are excluded.

The phrase **audit of accounts** is regulated in Spain. Do not use the visibility-audit service to review accounts, financial statements or issue assurance intended to have effects against third parties.

## 2. Mandatory sequence before the first paid client

### Gate 1 — choose the operating form

Record whether the activity will begin as:

- a self-employed natural person (`autónomo`); or
- a Spanish company or other legal entity.

The decision record must include the operating address, effective date, owner and reason. It must not be inferred from the website or GitHub account.

### Gate 2 — determine the municipal route

Use the Generalitat de Catalunya **FUE guided procedure** for the actual municipality and address. The answer depends on the place, whether clients attend, signage, works, employees and the use of a home or office.

Do not assume that a remote activity needs no municipal procedure. Record the guided-search result or a municipal confirmation.

Official reference:

- https://canalempresa.gencat.cat/es/ajuda/ajuda/ajuda-a-larea-privada/cerca-tramitacio-unificada/

### Gate 3 — file Modelo 036

The census registration must be filed before starting economic activity, performing operations or creating withholding obligations. Since February 2025, Modelo 037 is no longer the simplified alternative; the process uses Modelo 036.

The official receipt stays in restricted Drive storage. Source control may contain only the control ID, filing date and verified/not-verified state.

Official references:

- https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/censos-nif-domicilio-fiscal/tramites-censales-relacionados-empresarios-profesionales-retenedores/preguntas-frecuentes-modelos-036-037.html
- https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/calendario-contribuyente/calendario-contribuyente-2026/informacion-sobre-presentacion-modelos-no-periodicos/modelo-036.html

### Gate 4 — confirm IAE activity classification

Select the economic activity heading or headings that cover the actual mix of digital consultancy, CRM implementation, website work and commercial-content production.

Natural persons are exempt from paying IAE. Companies and other qualifying entities below EUR 1,000,000 net turnover are generally exempt from payment, but the activity still has to be identified through the applicable census process.

Do not commit an exact heading until the frozen professional confirmation is reopened or an official tax-assistance result is obtained.

Official references:

- https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/folleto-actividades-economicas/2-impuesto-sobre-actividades-economicas.html
- https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/declaraciones-informativas-otros-impuestos-tasas/impuesto-sobre-actividades-economicas/tengo-que-presentar-declaracion-iae.html

### Gate 5 — complete RETA or entity formation

When operating as an `autónomo`, complete Social Security registration through Importass before habitual self-employed activity begins. When operating through a company, complete the entity formation, tax and Social Security route that applies to the owner/administrator.

Official references:

- https://revista.seg-social.es/-/guia-para-darte-de-alta-en-la-seguridad-social-como-trabajador-autonomo
- https://www.seg-social.es/wps/portal/wss/internet/InformacionUtil/44539/44113/44128?changeLanguage=es

### Gate 6 — configure invoicing

Before issuing the first invoice, establish:

- invoice numbering and series;
- required issuer/customer/tax fields;
- VAT treatment and any cross-border rule;
- rectification process;
- invoice and supporting-document retention;
- the electronic-invoice route applicable to B2B operations.

Spanish businesses and professionals generally have to issue invoices for services and retain them. Spain published the B2B electronic-invoicing development regulation in March 2026; the exact implementation path and dates for this venture remain part of the frozen professional invoicing review.

Official references:

- https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/facturacion-registro/facturacion-iva/obligacion-facturar.html
- https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/facturacion-registro/facturacion-iva/contenido-facturas.html
- https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-noticias/2026/marzo/31/facturacion-electronica-obligatoria.html

### Gate 7 — complete website operator information

A commercial website and electronic contracting flow must display the operator identity and other required information. The LSSI does not impose a general prior authorisation for an online service, but it does not remove activity-specific, tax, consumer, privacy or municipal duties.

The current staged site deliberately contains no final identity, no real intake endpoint and no contracting mechanism.

Official reference:

- https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2002-13758

### Gate 8 — approve data and AI controls

Before collecting prospect data for the venture's own purposes, complete controller records, lawful basis, notices, retention, rights and security controls.

Before accessing a client's CRM under the client's instructions, execute an Article 28 processor agreement covering instructions, confidentiality, security, subprocessors, assistance and deletion/return.

Before live AI interaction, record provider/deployer roles, AI literacy, human oversight and Article 50 transparency. Relevant AI Act transparency obligations apply from 2 August 2026.

Official references:

- https://www.aepd.es/preguntas-frecuentes/2-tus-obligaciones-como-responsable-del-tratamiento/8-responsable-y-encargado-del-tratamiento/FAQ-0251-como-se-si-soy-responsable-o-encargado
- https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/guidelines-transparency-obligations-providers-and-deployers-ai-systems

## 3. Conditional controls

### EU clients or suppliers

Confirm place-of-supply, reverse-charge and ROI/VIES treatment before relevant intra-EU transactions. ROI registration is made through Modelo 036 when required.

### Consumers

If a natural person buys outside their trade or profession, activate consumer pre-contract, withdrawal, complaint and Catalonia official complaint-form controls. These are not treated as active while the launch remains B2B-only and non-transactional.

### Physical premises

A client-facing office, signage, works or employees may change the municipal procedure. Re-run the FUE path before any change.

### Insurance

Professional-indemnity and cyber insurance were not identified as mandatory for the current bounded scope, but should be quoted before handling material client data or integrations.

## 4. Official evidence custody

Store official documents only in restricted Drive storage. Required register fields:

- control ID;
- authority;
- filing or decision date;
- effective date;
- restricted receipt location;
- reviewer;
- state;
- expiry/review date;
- notes.

Never put NIF, home address, bank details, signed tax filings, Social Security receipts or professional opinions in public source control.
