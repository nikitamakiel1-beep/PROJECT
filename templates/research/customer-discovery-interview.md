## Opening

Explain that the purpose is to understand current international visibility, sales follow-up and commercial-document problems. State that the conversation is not a commitment to buy or sell and that confidential information is not required.

## Core questions

1. What are you currently trying to improve in international sales or visibility?
2. What triggered the need now?
3. How do enquiries, prospects and follow-ups move through the business today?
4. Where does information get lost, delayed or duplicated?
5. What do international prospects most often misunderstand or question?
6. What have you already tried? What worked and what failed?
7. What does this problem cost in time, missed opportunities, rework or management attention?
8. Who owns the problem and who approves a solution?
9. What evidence would make a small fixed-scope service credible?
10. What delivery format and timing would be practical?
11. What price range would be plausible for a bounded service, if any?
12. Who else experiences this problem or could provide a different perspective?

Do not lead the participant toward IVA, CRM or IOP. Capture their language before mapping it to service concepts.

## Commercial boundary

- No proposal sent during the interview.
- No automatic follow-up generated.
- Any later contact requires a human decision, a valid contact basis and a separate CRM activity.
- Any price discussed is research evidence, not an offer.

## Close

Ask permission for one clarification contact. Ask whether the participant knows another suitable interviewee. Do not request a testimonial.
