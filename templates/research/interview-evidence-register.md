## No names or direct identifiers

Do not include:

- participant or company names;
- email addresses or telephone numbers;
- street addresses;
- unredacted URLs tied to a private participant;
- personal opinions attributed to an identifiable individual;
- credentials, account references or commercially confidential records;
- recording links or raw transcript links in public source control.

## Evidence-quality scale

- `0 — unusable`: incomplete, unclear source or no permission basis.
- `1 — anecdotal`: one unverified statement without operational detail.
- `2 — supported`: concrete example, current workaround or observable consequence.
- `3 — strong`: repeated problem, measurable consequence, decision access or willingness-to-act evidence.

## Completion gate

The ten-interview target is complete only when:

- ten valid pseudonymous records exist;
- the required segment mix is represented;
- repeated and contradictory themes are both recorded;
- no automatic outreach or proposal was generated;
- a human has reviewed the aggregate themes;
- the CRM contains only the contact and activity data permitted by the chosen contact basis.
