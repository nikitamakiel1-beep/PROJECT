## Layout specification

The final page should contain:

- one headline;
- no more than 550 words per language unless an exception is approved;
- one primary call to action;
- no more than three main benefits;
- one proof section;
- adequate white space and readable contrast;
- descriptive links rather than raw URLs where possible;
- contact information consistent with the verified business identity.

Do not force two full languages onto one visually unreadable page. A bilingual delivery may use front/back or parallel versions if approved.

## Variants

### Prospect version

Prioritise buyer problem, offer, proof and direct next step.

### Distributor/partner version

Prioritise market fit, commercial model, operational reliability, partner role and qualification route.

Do not combine incompatible audiences in one page without a deliberate hierarchy.
