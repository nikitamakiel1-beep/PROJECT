## Scope and limitations

**Scope:** public-facing website and publicly available commercial material supplied or approved by the client.

**Excluded:** legal review, accessibility certification, security testing, SEO guarantee, paid-ad performance analysis, tax advice and verification of claims that cannot be evidenced from accepted sources.

The audit records a time-bounded commercial assessment. Websites change; evidence must include the capture date and source location.

## Rating interpretation

| Score | Interpretation | Required response |
|---:|---|---|
| 0–39 | Material commercial friction | Correct foundational clarity and trust failures before acquisition spend. |
| 40–59 | Partially credible but inconsistent | Resolve the highest-friction journey and localisation gaps. |
| 60–79 | Credible baseline with conversion leakage | Prioritise proof, calls to action and market-specific adaptation. |
| 80–100 | Strong visible readiness | Validate with real prospect behaviour and controlled experiments. |

Scores are diagnostic, not guarantees of revenue or market acceptance.

## Area analysis

### 1. Offer clarity — /20

Assess:

- visitor can identify the business, audience and outcome within the first screen;
- service names and descriptions use buyer language;
- primary offer and next step are unambiguous;
- exclusions or constraints do not contradict the offer;
- price or qualification expectations are appropriately signposted.

### 2. International readiness — /20

Assess:

- market and audience are named or inferable;
- delivery geography and operational constraints are clear;
- currency, units, dates and contact expectations fit the target audience;
- international enquiries have a credible route;
- unsupported claims such as “global” or “world-leading” are absent or evidenced.

**Evidence / finding / correction:** [ ]

### 3. Trust and proof — /20

Assess:

- legal/trading identity and contact details are coherent;
- team, process, examples or credentials support the offer;
- testimonials or logos have a verifiable permission basis;
- claims are specific and evidenced;
- privacy, security and professional boundaries are not overstated.

**Evidence / finding / correction:** [ ]

### 4. Conversion path — /20

Assess:

- one primary call to action is prominent;
- forms request proportionate information;
- confirmation and response expectations are clear;
- mobile journey is usable;
- broken links, dead ends and contradictory calls to action are absent.

**Evidence / finding / correction:** [ ]

### 5. Language and localisation — /10

Assess:

- selected languages are complete rather than partially translated;
- tone and terminology fit the intended buyer;
- language switching preserves route and meaning;
- untranslated legal, technical or navigation fragments are identified;
- machine translation is human-reviewed before publication.

**Evidence / finding / correction:** [ ]

### 6. Technical baseline — /10

Commercial surface checks only:

- HTTPS and obvious browser warnings;
- responsive layout and readable contrast;
- working navigation and forms;
- descriptive page titles and basic metadata;
- reasonable visible performance and media behaviour.

This is not a penetration test, accessibility audit or Core Web Vitals certification.
