## 1. Parties and authority

Required decisions:

- verified provider legal/trading identity;
- verified client identity and authorised representative;
- public and contractual contact details;
- applicable legal form and registration disclosures.

No identifiers or private addresses belong in this public template.

## 2. Contract formation

Define:

- documents forming the agreement;
- precedence between proposal, statement of work and terms;
- acceptable approval method;
- proposal validity period;
- point at which work is authorised to begin.

## 3. Scope and exclusions

The proposal must state the exact deliverable, included elements, exclusions, client inputs, delivery format and revision limit. Work outside those boundaries requires a written change request.

## 4. Client responsibilities

Define client responsibility for:

- accurate and timely inputs;
- rights to use supplied content, logos, data and materials;
- secure credential sharing;
- consolidated feedback;
- approval authority;
- lawful instructions and use of the deliverable.

## 5. Price, taxes and payment

Blocking decisions:

- verified price and currency;
- tax/VAT treatment;
- invoice issuer and numbering process;
- deposit/prepayment requirement;
- payment deadline;
- accepted payment methods;
- late-payment treatment;
- third-party costs and approval threshold;
- refund handling.

The repository must never contain bank details, tax identifiers or live payment credentials.

## 6. Delivery timing and dependency delay

Define:

- working-day convention and time zone;
- start condition;
- effect of late or defective client inputs;
- client review window;
- treatment of force majeure or third-party platform failure;
- rescheduling process.

## 7. Revisions and change control

The initial service includes one consolidated revision round unless the canonical service definition states otherwise. Define:

- what constitutes correction versus new scope;
- feedback deadline;
- handling of fragmented feedback;
- additional-round price/rate;
- requirement for written approval before extra work.

## 8. Acceptance and defects

Define objective acceptance criteria and a reasonable defect-reporting window. Distinguish:

- factual or technical defect;
- failure to meet agreed scope;
- preference change;
- new information supplied after approval;
- third-party platform behaviour outside control.

Do not rely on deemed acceptance without legal review.

## 9. Cancellation, pause and termination

Blocking decisions:

- cancellation before work starts;
- cancellation after work starts;
- treatment of completed work and non-refundable costs;
- provider suspension for non-payment, unlawful instruction or missing input;
- termination for material breach;
- return/deletion of client material;
- survival of payment, confidentiality and intellectual-property clauses.

## 10. Intellectual property and permitted use

Define:

- ownership of client-supplied materials;
- ownership/licence of the final deliverable;
- ownership of pre-existing templates, methods and generic know-how;
- transfer condition, including payment completion;
- third-party licences;
- portfolio/publication permission as separate opt-in approval.

No client name, logo, testimonial or result may be published without written permission.

## 11. Confidentiality and data protection

Define confidentiality scope, permitted disclosure, security expectations and return/deletion. Refer to the approved privacy and processor records where personal data is processed. Credentials require a secure channel and minimum access.

## 12. Warranties and professional boundaries

The service is a commercial/operational deliverable. It does not replace legal, tax, accounting, cybersecurity, accessibility-certification or regulated-professional advice. Define any reasonable service warranty without guaranteeing revenue, ranking, conversion or market acceptance.

## 13. Liability and indemnity

Requires jurisdiction-specific professional review. Record decisions on:

- excluded loss categories where lawful;
- liability cap and exceptions;
- client responsibility for supplied materials and instructions;
- third-party services;
- non-excludable liability.

No liability wording may be issued from this scaffold without review.

## 14. Governing law and dispute process

Blocking decisions:

- governing law;
- courts or alternative dispute process;
- consumer/business applicability;
- mandatory local rules;
- notice method.
