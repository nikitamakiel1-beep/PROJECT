## Qualification dimensions

Score each dimension from 0 to 2 and record one evidence sentence.

Maximum score: 16.

## Disqualifying conditions

Disqualify or hold when any applies:

- the requested outcome requires legal, tax, cybersecurity, accessibility-certification or regulated professional advice;
- the prospect requests fabricated evidence, fake testimonials, deceptive localisation or unsupported claims;
- required access would expose credentials through an unapproved channel;
- the scope cannot fit the canonical service boundary and no human-approved change order exists;
- the lead source or contact basis is unclear;
- the repository or provider safety gate blocks use of live identifiers;
- the prospect requests payment, invoice or contractual treatment not yet professionally verified.
