## Data minimisation

- Do not provide passwords, private keys, payment-card data or unrestricted account access.
- Redact customer, employee and supplier personal data unless explicitly necessary and approved.
- Prefer screenshots, exports or temporary least-privilege access over permanent credentials.
- Use fictional or synthetic data for demonstrations and tests.
- Remove hidden comments, revision history and metadata when they are outside scope.
- Do not upload confidential material to public repositories or public links.

## Secure access rules

Client credentials must be shared only through the Approved secure channel recorded above. Email, chat and shared documents must not contain passwords or recovery codes. Temporary access must have:

- least privilege;
- named owner;
- expiry date;
- revocation check after delivery;
- incident contact.

## Input deadline and delivery clock

The delivery clock begins only when all mandatory inputs are received, readable and verified. Missing, contradictory or unsafe inputs pause the clock. Any material scope expansion requires a human-approved change request.
