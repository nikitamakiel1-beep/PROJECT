## Scope and commercial control

- [ ] Service code, price, delivery date and revision limit match the approved proposal or demonstration brief.
- [ ] Every included item is present.
- [ ] No excluded item has been represented as included.
- [ ] Any scope change has written approval and an updated commercial record.
- [ ] Required payment and invoice gates are satisfied before final client delivery.
- [ ] The deliverable does not imply legal, tax, accounting, cybersecurity or certification advice.

## Evidence and factual accuracy

- [ ] Names, URLs, product names, figures and dates are verified against the accepted source.
- [ ] Scores, calculations and totals reproduce from the recorded inputs.
- [ ] Sources are recorded where a claim depends on external evidence.
- [ ] Observations are separated from assumptions, interpretations and recommendations.
- [ ] No unsupported testimonial, client result, market claim or performance guarantee appears.
- [ ] Demonstration content is visibly labelled as fictional or public-example material.

## Privacy, confidentiality and access

- [ ] Only minimum necessary personal data appears.
- [ ] No credentials, tokens, spreadsheet IDs, private deployment URLs or personal contact data appear in public artifacts.
- [ ] Client-provided material is used within the confirmed permission boundary.
- [ ] Sensitive material is stored only in the approved client folder.
- [ ] Sharing permissions are restricted to the intended recipients.
- [ ] Retention or deletion date is recorded where required.

## Content and usability

- [ ] Language, spelling, tone and terminology are reviewed.
- [ ] English/Spanish content is complete where bilingual delivery was purchased.
- [ ] Headings, tables, links and page breaks render correctly.
- [ ] Links open and point to the intended destination.
- [ ] Editable and final formats match the proposal.
- [ ] Instructions can be followed by a person who did not build the deliverable.
- [ ] Accessibility basics are checked: readable contrast, meaningful headings, descriptive link text and non-colour-only status indicators.

## Service-specific verification

### IVA

- [ ] Weighted score totals exactly 100 points.
- [ ] Every score has an evidence reference.
- [ ] Priority actions include owner, effort, impact and 30-day sequence.
- [ ] Limitations distinguish visibility review from professional certification.

### CRM

- [ ] Pipeline stages and terminal states are defined.
- [ ] Required fields, ownership and follow-up rules are explicit.
- [ ] Duplicate, backup, import and rollback procedures are documented.
- [ ] Dashboard definitions reconcile to the underlying fields.

### IOP

- [ ] Audience, problem, offer, proof and call to action form one coherent narrative.
- [ ] Claims are supported or qualified.
- [ ] Localisation notes identify untranslated legal or technical terms.
- [ ] Final copy fits the agreed one-page constraint or records an approved exception.

## File and handover integrity

- [ ] Files open without error.
- [ ] File names follow the controlled naming convention.
- [ ] Final and editable versions are clearly distinguished.
- [ ] The client folder contains the proposal, accepted inputs, draft history, final files and this checklist.
- [ ] Actual delivery time, direct cost, revision count and completion date are recorded in the CRM.
- [ ] The handover message states what is delivered, what remains client-owned and how the revision window works.
