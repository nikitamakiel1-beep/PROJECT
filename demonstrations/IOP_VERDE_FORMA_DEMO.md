# Distinctive Spanish tableware for boutique hospitality spaces

## For hotels, restaurants and specialist distributors seeking small-batch collections with a clear sourcing process

Verde Forma Ceramics helps hospitality teams specify coordinated tableware without committing to industrial-scale volumes. Each fictional collection is produced in Spain using lead-free glazes, with a structured sample, specification and production process.

## A practical response to generic or inflexible tableware sourcing

Boutique hospitality projects often need visual distinction, manageable order quantities and a supplier who can explain what is standard, what can be adapted and when production can realistically be completed. Verde Forma provides a focused catalogue and a controlled route for colour and collection decisions.

## What Verde Forma offers

- **Small-batch catalogue orders** from 60 mixed pieces.
- **Coordinated forms and finishes** for restaurant, breakfast and in-room service concepts.
- **Custom colour development** after sample approval and technical confirmation.
- **Standard production lead time of six to eight weeks** after final specification.
- **Clear quotation boundaries** for product, packaging, samples and freight.

## Three concrete benefits

1. **Distinctive presentation without industrial order volumes** — the fictional minimum allows a buyer to combine approved catalogue pieces in one coordinated order.
2. **A controlled specification process** — forms, finishes, quantities and sample approval are documented before production begins.
3. **More predictable procurement conversations** — lead time, minimum order and separately quoted costs are stated before commitment.

## How engagement works

1. **Share the project brief:** service style, opening date, quantities, preferred forms and finish direction.
2. **Select and confirm samples:** choose catalogue references or approve a custom-colour development route.
3. **Approve the final specification and quotation:** production begins only after written approval and the agreed payment condition.

**Standard lead time:** six to eight weeks after final specification. Sample development, custom finishes, packaging and freight may affect timing and price.

## Start with a specification conversation

Send the intended opening date, approximate quantities and preferred collection direction. Verde Forma will confirm whether the fictional catalogue, minimum order and timeline fit the project before a quotation is prepared.
