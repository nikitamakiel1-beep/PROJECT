## Fictional business brief

Northline Process Instruments is a small Barcelona-area manufacturer of pressure and flow instrumentation for industrial distributors. The fictional company has a credible product range and technical knowledge, but its synthetic website was written mainly for existing Spanish-speaking contacts. It now wants to make first conversations with international distributors easier.

## Executive score

**57 / 100 — partially credible but inconsistent for an international visitor.**

Northline appears technically capable, but the fictional website does not explain its distributor proposition quickly enough. Product detail exists, yet proof, geography, English completeness and the enquiry route are fragmented. The recommended 30-day objective is to make one international distributor journey complete from landing page to qualified enquiry.

## Findings

### 1. Offer clarity — 13/20

The fictional first screen states “Industrial measurement solutions” and lists pressure, flow and temperature families. It does not say which buyer it serves, whether it manufactures or distributes, or why an international partner should continue.

**Correction:** replace the generic headline with a distributor-oriented outcome and one qualification sentence, for example: “Process instrumentation for European industrial distributors — configurable pressure and flow solutions with documented technical support.”

### 2. International readiness — 9/20

The synthetic English navigation is complete, but several product descriptions return to Spanish. Delivery regions, lead times, minimum order expectations and export-document availability are absent.

**Correction:** publish one complete English distributor path that states target regions, typical response time, available documentation and the first qualification step. Avoid claiming global coverage until operational evidence exists.

### 3. Trust and proof — 12/20

The fictional site shows factory photography, technical datasheets and a quality statement. It also displays two certification abbreviations without a verification link or scope statement.

**Correction:** create a compact proof block with verified certification names, scope, issuing body, current date and source file. Add a labelled demonstration application example rather than an invented customer logo.

### 4. Conversion path — 11/20

A general contact form and email are available. The form asks for name, company and message, but does not ask market, product family, application or expected volume. No response time or privacy acknowledgement appears in the synthetic brief.

**Correction:** add a distributor enquiry route with proportionate fields and a clear response expectation. Real activation remains blocked until approved privacy wording and provider evidence exist.

### 5. Language and localisation — 5/10

English headings are readable, but product attributes mix comma/decimal conventions and several PDF titles remain Spanish-only.

**Correction:** maintain a controlled terminology list and verify units, decimal formats, product-family names and legal/technical terms. Provide parallel files only where an English source has been reviewed.

### 6. Technical baseline — 7/10

The synthetic mobile layout is usable. A full-width factory video loads above product categories and delays the first commercial message. Light-theme secondary text has weak contrast.

**Correction:** use a static poster or deferred video, move product/distributor value above the fold and test contrast in both themes.

## Quick wins

1. Replace “Industrial measurement solutions” with an audience-specific headline.
2. Add “Response within two working days” only after the team confirms it can meet that commitment.
3. Add verified region, language and product-family filters to the contact path.

## Medium-term actions

1. Build a distributor resource pack containing reviewed datasheets, application notes and certification evidence.
2. Introduce structured enquiry routing only after privacy and provider activation gates pass.
3. Validate the new page through five real distributor conversations before increasing acquisition spend.

## Management one-page summary

**Current position:** Northline's fictional technical credibility is stronger than its international commercial narrative.

**Three decisions:**

1. Select one priority international distributor audience.
2. Publish one complete English journey rather than translating fragments.
3. Require evidence custody for every certification and performance claim.

**Expected 30-day evidence:** one reviewed landing page, one verified proof register, one controlled enquiry design and five documented discovery conversations.
