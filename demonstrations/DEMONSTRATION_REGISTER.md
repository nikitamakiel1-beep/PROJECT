# Controlled demonstration register

> Every item in this directory is fictional and exists only to demonstrate method, format and quality controls. No item represents a real client, testimonial, performance result or completed commercial engagement.

## Public labelling rule

Each demonstration must display, near the title:

> **Demonstration — fictional business and synthetic evidence. Not a client result.**

The label must remain visible in derived PDFs, screenshots, website cards and portfolio previews.

## Demonstration controls

- All businesses, people, domains, transactions and results are fictional.
- Example emails use reserved invalid domains such as `.example` or are omitted.
- No client name, logo, screenshot, testimonial or identifiable record is reused.
- Numerical evidence is synthetic and labelled.
- Demonstrations may show the method, not claim market performance.
- Public website wording must say `Demonstration`, never `Case study`, unless a real client separately approves a truthful case study.
- The canonical service price, scope and revision limit must match `schemas/services.json`.
- A human completes `templates/commercial/quality-checklist.md` before public publication.

## Publication checklist

- [ ] Demonstration label appears at the title and final callout.
- [ ] Fictional status survives PDF/image export.
- [ ] No real domain, person, email, phone, address or identifier appears.
- [ ] No invented testimonial or revenue/conversion result appears.
- [ ] Service scope and price are canonical.
- [ ] Claims are limited to the demonstrated method and synthetic findings.
- [ ] Links and assets are safe for public distribution.
- [ ] Human reviewer approves the exact public version.
